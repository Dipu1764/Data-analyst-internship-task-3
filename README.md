# Data-analyst-internship-task-3

 **Book Sales Analysis Dashboard (Tableau)**
 
**📂 Project Overview**
This project analyzes book sales data using Tableau. It focuses on key performance indicators (KPIs), author and genre analysis, sales performance, and publishing trends to uncover insights that can help publishers and sellers improve business decisions.

**🧾 Dataset Description**
The dataset contains the following fields:

**Column Name	Description**
index	Unique identifier for each record
Publishing Year	Year the book was published
Book Name	Title of the book
Author	Name of the author
language_code	Language in which the book is published
Author_Rating	Average rating of the author
Book_average_rating	Average rating of the book
Book_ratings_count	Number of ratings received by the book
genre	Book genre (e.g., Fiction, Non-fiction, Romance)
gross sales	Total gross sales value
publisher revenue	Revenue generated by the publisher
sale price	Price at which the book was sold
sales rank	Sales rank of the book
Publisher	Name of the publishing company
units sold	Total number of units sold

**📈 Visualizations (Charts)**
#	Chart Title	Type	Insight
1	Sales by Year	Line Chart	Track total sales over years
2	Genre-wise Sales	Bar Chart	Compare genre popularity
3	Top Authors by Units Sold	Horizontal Bar	Identify top-performing authors
4	Average Rating by Genre	Bubble Chart	Discover quality trends per genre
5	Revenue by Publisher	TreeMap	Visualize publisher contribution
6	Language-wise Book Count	Pie Chart	See language distribution
7	Author Rating vs Units Sold	Scatter Plot	Analyze correlation between author rating and sales
8	Publisher Revenue by Year	Area Chart	Check revenue trends
9	Best-Selling Books	Table	Highlight top-performing titles
10	Average Sale Price by Genre	Bar Chart	Understand pricing strategies

These filters help interactively explore the dataset based on specific parameters.

**💡 Insights**
Fiction and Romance are the highest-grossing genres.

Authors with higher ratings tend to sell more units.

Top 5 publishers account for over 70% of total revenue.

Books with a higher rating count tend to rank better in sales.

Publishing year trends show increasing sales post-2010.

**✅ Conclusion & Recommendations**
Focus more on high-performing genres like Fiction and Romance.
Invest in top authors with strong historical performance and ratings.
Use dynamic pricing based on genre and performance to optimize revenue.
Expand offerings in high-demand languages to reach broader audiences.
Improve marketing strategies for underperforming publishers or genres.
